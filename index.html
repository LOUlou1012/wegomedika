Why Wego Medika

A trusted partner in medical equipment procurement

We are here to meet the need for high-quality medical equipment at competitive prices, reaching every segment of healthcare services in Indonesia.

Proven Experience & Reputation

Since 2012, Wego Medika Indonesia has served as a medical device distributor linking world-class manufacturers with hospitals, clinics, and laboratories throughout Indonesia. Our focus has stayed on high-quality diagnostic, cardiovascular, and medical disposable products.

Wide Geographic Reach

Our distribution network reaches more than 34 cities across Indonesia, ensuring products arrive for both national needs and market penetration in your region.

Relevant Product Portfolio

Our product range covers laboratory diagnostics, cardiovascular devices, and medical disposables — tailored to the specific needs of your healthcare facility.

Regulatory Compliance

We have a deep understanding of local regulations and standards, ensuring every product we distribute meets BPOM requirements and faces no market-entry barriers.

After-Sales Support

Our customer service team is ready to handle any issue quickly and precisely, maintaining customer satisfaction and our partners' reputation.

Fast & Precise Delivery

A fast and accurate shipping process, ensuring products arrive on time and to specification at your facility.
